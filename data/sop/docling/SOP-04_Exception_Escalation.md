## Exception and Escalation Policy

Document: SOP-04

Version: v2.3

Effective date: 2025-10-01

Owner: Import Operations, Meridian Freight Co.

Approved by: Operations Manager

## 1 Exception Categories

## 1.1 Categories

The Import Operator must classify every exception arising on an import file into one of the categories set out in the table below. Any event falling within these categories shall be handled under this policy.

| Exception Category           |
|------------------------------|
| Vessel delay > 3 days        |
| Customs inspection           |
| Document discrepancy         |
| Demurrage/detention exposure |
| Cargo damage or shortage     |
| Regulatory hold              |

## 1.2 Severity

The Import Operator must assign a severity level to each exception at the time it is identified. An exception shall be classified as Severity 1 where it carries a potential cost over USD 500 or a delivery delay over 5 days. All other exceptions shall be classified as Severity 2.

## 2 Reporting

## 2.1 Consignee notification

The Import Operator must inform the consignee of any exception within 1 working day of Meridian becoming aware of it. This notification obligation applies to all exceptions regardless of severity.

## 2.2 Incident record

The Import Operator must log each exception in the file. Each log entry shall record the date detected, the cause, the expected impact and the owner of the exception.

## 3 Escalation

## 3.1 48-hour escalation

The Import Operator must escalate every Severity 1 exception to the Import Team Lead in writing. Such escalation shall be made within 48 hours of detection.

## 3.2 Management escalation

The Import Team Lead must escalate to the Operations Manager any exception with a potential cost over USD 2,000 or involving a claim against Meridian. This escalation is in addition to the escalation required for Severity 1 exceptions.

## 3.3 Decision recording

Every escalation must end with a recorded decision. The person who made the decision shall ensure that the record states what was decided, by whom and on which date.

## 4 Claims

## 4.1 Claim notice

Where cargo damage or shortage is noted at delivery, the Import Operator must notify the carrier in writing within 3 days of delivery. This notice is required to preserve Meridian's recourse against the carrier.

## 4.2 Evidence

The Import Operator must collect photos and the delivery receipt remarks before any settlement discussion takes place. Where the value is over USD 10,000, a survey report shall also be obtained before any settlement discussion.
