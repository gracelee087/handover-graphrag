## Customs Broker and Vendor Management Policy

Document: SOP-05

Version: v1.4

Effective date: 2025-04-01

Owner: Import Operations, Meridian Freight Co.

Approved by: Operations Manager

## 1 Approved Vendors

## 1.1 Approved list

Only customs brokers and truckers that appear on the approved vendor list may be instructed to perform services on behalf of Meridian Freight Co. The Import Operator must verify that a vendor is on the approved vendor list before issuing any instruction. The approved vendor list shall be maintained by the Operations Manager.

## 1.2 Broker allocation

Customs brokers shall be allocated to customers as set out in the following table, and the Import Operator must instruct the broker assigned to the relevant customer.

## 2 Instruction and Quotation

## 2.1 Written instruction

The Import Operator must instruct brokers and truckers by e-mail, and the HBL number shall be stated in the subject line of each instruction. Where an instruction is given verbally, the Import Operator must confirm it in writing on the same day.

## 2.2 Rate validity

Vendor rates shall be agreed on an annual basis and recorded in the rate sheet. Any charge falling outside the rate sheet requires a quotation, which must be approved by the Import Team Lead before the service is performed. The Import Operator must not authorise a vendor to proceed with such a service until this approval has been obtained.

## 3 Performance Review

## 3.1 Quarterly review

The Operations Manager shall review vendor performance each quarter. This review must cover broker clearance lead time and trucker on-time rate.

## 3.2 Non-conformance

Repeated errors by a vendor must be recorded and discussed with the vendor concerned. Where two non-conformances are recorded against a vendor in a quarter, the Operations Manager shall initiate a review of that vendor's allocation.

## 4 Invoices

## 4.1 Invoice check

The Import Operator must check each vendor invoice against the original instruction and the rate sheet. Accounting shall not pay a vendor invoice until this check has been completed by the Import Operator.
